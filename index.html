ExperienceWelcome to the DBA

From Executive Experience to Applied Impact

The Doctor of Business Administration at Al Yamamah University, offered by the College of Business, is designed for experienced business leaders, professional executives, and government officials who wish to connect advanced business knowledge with rigorous applied research and strategic leadership.

The program develops the ability to investigate complex organizational and public-sector challenges through evidence-based decision-making, ethical leadership, innovation, governance, analytics, and applied research.

With a strong practice orientation, the DBA supports the transformation ambitions of Saudi Vision 2030 and the changing needs of Saudi Arabia’s economy. Candidates are expected to connect their professional experience with a significant real-world challenge and develop research-driven recommendations with practical value.

Mission

To empower business leaders, professional executives, and government officials with a high-quality education that advances their careers, drives impactful research, and fosters transformative societal change.

ExperienceWho the program is for

Designed for Experienced Leaders

Choose the context closest to your own. The program is built for people who already lead, and who want to investigate a consequential challenge with doctoral rigor.

The DBA is intended for professionals who:

  • Hold a master’s degree or equivalent.
  • Have substantial professional and leadership experience.
  • Want to investigate a consequential organizational, managerial, sector, or policy challenge.
  • Need advanced research capability for evidence-based decision-making.
  • Seek to lead transformation, innovation, governance, or organizational improvement.
  • Can commit to intensive on-campus residency periods and supervised research.

KnowledgeExecutive format and delivery

Built Around an Executive Journey

The DBA follows a sequenced study model that supports professional commitments while maintaining doctoral continuity, academic rigor, supervision, and research progress.

Coursework is normally organized around intensive Thursday-to-Saturday residency periods, supported by scheduled live online sessions between residencies where published in the official cohort calendar.

KnowledgeProgram architecture

42 Credits. One Integrated Journey.

The DBA consists of 42 required credit hours. The first four semesters complete 30 coursework credits, followed by the 12-credit Applied Research Project in the final two semesters.

The approved program contains no electives or major tracks.

ResearchThe Applied Research Project

From a Real-World Challenge to Applied Impact

The Applied Research Project is a mandatory, supervised investigation that turns a consequential real-world challenge into credible evidence and actionable recommendations.

The project is expected to demonstrate methodological rigor, practical relevance, ethical conduct, and potential value for an organization, industry, public-sector body, or broader professional context.

Progression gate

Before registering for DBA 611, candidates must:

  • Complete 30 coursework credits.
  • Hold a cumulative GPA of at least 3.00/4.00.
  • Successfully defend the research proposal.
  • Have an approved research topic.
  • Have approved supervision arrangements.

ResearchSupervision and quality assurance

Supervision and Academic Quality

The Applied Research Project is supported through an approved dual-supervision model. At least two qualified supervisors provide complementary subject, methodological, or sector expertise.

Qualified internal and external examiners assess the project’s rigor, originality, practical relevance, and contribution.

Dual supervision · at least two qualified supervisors

Gate 1 of 6

Topic approval

Candidates begin exploring an applied research topic in Year 1. Supervisor nomination and formal topic approval are expected by the end of the first year.

Gate 2 of 6

Supervisor matching

Matching considers the candidate’s research topic, research method, sector context, available academic expertise, and supervisor capacity.

Gate 3 of 6

Ethics approval

Research ethics review is part of the program’s academic quality framework. Required approvals are secured in Part I of the Applied Research Project (DBA 611).

Gate 4 of 6

Research progress

Scheduled supervision meetings, periodic progress reports, research seminars, documented feedback, and academic integrity and similarity checking support steady progress.

Gate 5 of 6

Examination

Internal and external examination. Qualified internal and external examiners assess the project’s rigor, originality, practical relevance, and contribution.

Gate 6 of 6

Final approval

The final viva voce. Required corrections are addressed and the final research project is submitted.

Admission Requirements

Eligibility is assessed consistently against documented academic, professional, English-language, recommendation, research, and interview requirements.

DBA admission requirements and published baselines
RequirementPublished baseline
Academic qualificationMaster’s degree or equivalent from an accredited institution recognized by the Ministry of Education
Minimum GPA3.00/4.00 or 3.75/5.00
Professional experienceMinimum of 7 years, including at least 3 years in leadership. Applicants with 10 years or more are preferred.
English proficiencyTOEFL 550 or above, TOEFL iBT 79 or above, or IELTS 6.0 or above, with a minimum score of 6.0 in each component
RecommendationsTwo confidential letters of recommendation, preferably from academic instructors or senior executives
Research proposalAn applied research proposal or research statement of 1,500–2,500 words
InterviewShortlisted candidates participate in an interview conducted by the DBA Program Committee

Important admissions note

Meeting the minimum requirements establishes eligibility but does not guarantee admission.

Selection also considers research readiness, leadership evidence, program fit, supervision fit, and cohort capacity.

ApplicationHow to apply

DBA Admissions Journey

Six stages, from a complete submission to an official decision. Applications are submitted through the official Graduate Admissions portal.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Submit Application

    Complete the official online application and upload all required documents.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Eligibility Screening

    Graduate Admissions confirms that the application is complete and meets the published minimum requirements.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Academic Evaluation

    The DBA Program Director evaluates the academic record, professional experience, leadership background, research proposal, and readiness for doctoral study.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Program Committee Interview

    Shortlisted candidates participate in a structured interview with the DBA Program Committee.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Committee Recommendation & Approval

    Successful cases are formally recommended and forwarded through the approved graduate studies governance process.

  6. 06

    Step 6: Admission Outcome

    Graduate Admissions communicates the official decision and provides enrolment, registration, and orientation instructions.
